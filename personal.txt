4. Most important part: crash safety

We must not do this:

write directly to snapshot
        ↓
crash halfway
        ↓
corrupted snapshot 💀

Instead:

write snapshot.tmp
       ↓
Sync()
       ↓
rename snapshot.tmp → snapshot

So we either have:

old valid snapshot

or:

new valid snapshot

rather than a half-written snapshot.

This is a very good production-style concept to discuss in an interview.
